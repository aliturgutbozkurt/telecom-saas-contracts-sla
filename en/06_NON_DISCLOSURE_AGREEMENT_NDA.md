# TelcoPulse Omni-BSS & eSIM Cloud Suite
## Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
**Document Code:** `EN-TELCO-NDA-2026-06`  
**Contract Reference:** `EN-TELCO-MSA-2026-01` Annex-5  
**Classification:** STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL / NETWORK SECURITY  
**Date:** October 18, 2026  

---

### 1. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" encompasses all proprietary technical, financial, and strategic information disclosed directly or indirectly between the Parties. In the telecommunications domain, this explicitly includes:

1. **Carrier Infrastructure Details:** Core network topologies, IP routing tables, cell tower coordinates, HSM cryptographic deployment blueprints, and Diameter/SS7 gateway peering configurations.
2. **Subscriber & Commercial Metrics:** Active subscriber base counts, churn rates, ARPU breakdowns, tariff formulas, and real-time charging algorithms.
3. **Software & Intellectual Assets:** TelcoPulse source code, container configurations, benchmark data, and patent-pending scheduling logic.

---

### 2. Standard of Care & Operational Restrictions

1. **Highest Degree of Care:** Each Party agrees to protect Confidential Information using the highest degree of care, and in no event less than reasonable care complying with ISO/IEC 27001 standards.
2. **Need-to-Know Access:** Dissemination is restricted to employees and vetted sub-contractors who require access for the execution of this contract and who have signed individual confidentiality undertakings.
3. **Prohibition on External Tools:** Under no circumstances shall proprietary code, configuration manifests, or telecom logs be uploaded to public repositories (e.g., GitHub public), developer forums, or public generative AI tools.

---

### 3. Liquidated Damages & Injunctive Relief

1. **Pre-Agreed Damages:** Any material breach of confidentiality gives rise to pre-agreed liquidated damages of **USD 500,000 (Five Hundred Thousand United States Dollars)** per occurrence, without prejudice to the non-breaching Party’s right to claim additional actual damages.
2. **Immediate Injunction:** The Parties acknowledge that breach of network security secrets causes irreparable injury. The non-breaching Party is entitled to seek immediate injunctive relief without posting bond.

---

### 4. Duration & Perpetuity

This Agreement remains binding for **five (5) years** following the termination of the Master Services Agreement; provided, however, that trade secrets, cryptographic keys, and critical network infrastructure topologies shall remain protected in **perpetuity**.
